# POSIX extraction in 3.0

`libpkgapply` 2.3.0 shipped two separately named products from one repository:
`libpkgapply.so.2` and `libpkgapply-posix.so.2`. The POSIX product already had
its own installed namespace, pkg-config file, manual, implementation directory,
and behavior suite.

The 3.0 generation moves that product to `libpkgapply-posix` without folding its
mechanisms into core policy or changing its SONAME generation. Core history
retains references to the earlier in-tree product; current build and source
ownership do not.
